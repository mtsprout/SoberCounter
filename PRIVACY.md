# Sobriety Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Sobriety Tracker counts the time since your sobriety date and reminds you when you reach goals you set. This policy explains what the app stores and where it goes.

**The short version:** the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The developer receives none of it.

## What the app stores

The following is saved only on your phone:

- Your sobriety date and time.
- The goals you add (for example "90 days" or "18 months").
- Optionally, a sponsor's name and phone number, if you add one.

The app never sends this information anywhere. You can delete it by changing or removing it in the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

**Android backup.** If Android's backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this app data in your device backup in your Google account, under Google's own terms. This lets the app's data move to a new phone.

## Permissions

- **Notifications.** Used to tell you when you reach a goal. You can decline this and the app still counts your time.
- **Run at startup.** Used to schedule your goal notifications again after your phone restarts.
- **Contacts.** The app does not ask for contacts permission. If you choose "Choose from contacts" when adding a sponsor, Android's own contact picker opens and the app receives only the one contact you pick.

## Sharing

The share button creates a picture of your year, month, day and hour counts. It does not include your sobriety date. The picture goes only to the app you choose in Android's share sheet, and is then governed by that app's terms.

## Links to other services

The Resources buttons open outside the app: AA (aa.org), NA (na.org) and SMART Recovery (smartrecovery.org) open in your browser, and the SAMHSA helpline and 988 buttons open your phone's dialer with the number filled in. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Sobriety Tracker is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these organizations.

## Not medical advice

Sobriety Tracker is a counter and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in the United States, or call SAMHSA's helpline at 1-800-662-4357.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
