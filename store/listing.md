## Full description (4000 max)
Count your sober time without giving anything away. Sobriety Tracker has no sign-in, no account and no ads. Your date stays on your phone.

Set the date and time your sobriety began and watch it grow in four glowing neon bars: Years, Months, Days and Hours. Each bar fills toward its next rollover, so you can see progress all day long.

GOALS THAT MATTER TO YOU
Set your own goals: 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 6 months, 18 months, a year, whatever you choose. You get a notification the moment you reach each one. A progress meter shows how far you are between your last goal and the next. When you add a goal, the app suggests your next milestone and won't let you set one you've already passed.

SUPPORT, ONE TAP AWAY
Quick links to AA, NA, SMART Recovery, the SAMHSA helpline and the 988 Lifeline. Add your sponsor's number, even straight from your contacts, so you can call when it counts.

SHARE YOUR PROGRESS
One tap makes a clean image of your years, months, days and hours to send to a friend, a sponsor or your group. Your sobriety date is not included.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No ads. No tracking. No data leaves your phone. The app asks only for permission to send you goal notifications.

Sobriety Tracker is a counter, not medical advice or treatment. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by AA, NA, SMART Recovery, SAMHSA or any other organization listed. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US).
